At BLS Language School in Phuket, you can obtain an ED visa at one of the most affordable prices on the island.

This student visa is available for those studying English, Thai, Chinese, or French and you can choose the language course that suits your goals best.

You can apply for a visa for 3, 6, 9, or 12 months. The cost depends on the duration of your studies. If you wish to stay longer, you can extend your ED visa by continuing your education with us — even by switching to another language program.

Frequently asked questions

Do you guarantee that you will receive a visa?

The school has the necessary quotas for obtaining a visa. 100% of our students who applied for a visa on time received it without having to make a border run.

When do I need to apply for a visa?

We recommend submitting documents 30–45 days before the expiration date of your current visa or entry stamp.

How long does it take to get a visa?

We will consider your application as soon as it is submitted. Obtaining a visa after signing a contract takes up to 30-35 days.

What if my visa stamp is less than 30 days from expiry?

The school will do everything possible to ensure you receive your visa quickly. If we have less time than needed, making a border run or extending your tourist visa will be necessary.

Is the visa retained when leaving the country?

A Re-Entry stamp must be obtained from the Immigration Office prior to departure to ensure that the visa is not canceled.

Do I need to apply for a visa for children?

There are no penalties for children under 14 years of age for being in Thailand without a visa. If necessary, we can arrange a student visa for children from 12 years old.

Can I learn several languages at once?

Yes, students enrolled in our programs may also attend other language courses.

What languages do you speak?

Our staff speaks Russian, Ukrainian, English, Thai and Chinese.

Do you provide preparation for international exams such as IELTS?

Yes, we offer optional IELTS and TOEFL preparation in addition to our main programs.

Can you help with questions not related to training or visa applications?

We are always willing to help our students with questions and share our expertise. Get in touch!
